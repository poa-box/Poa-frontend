01 / What Poa does

Work, membership, decisions, and shared funds.

Poa means Perpetual Organization Architect. The frontend supports organizations with membership and permissions, projects and reviewed tasks, contribution rewards called Shares, member voting, and funded distributions.

A good fit is a cooperative, collective, community group, or DAO willing to keep its operating records public and agree who may contribute, review work, make decisions, and receive funds. Public guides explain the product without sign-in; setup can be explored before launch.

What the copy must make clear

Shares are participation rights in Poa. They do not automatically grant legal equity or create a legally recognized cooperative. Payment requires funding and the relevant approval. Votes depend on eligibility; an approved action must also execute successfully.

Participation needs a passkey account or an existing wallet and appropriate authority. Network or external service costs may apply. Private client and member details belong in private tools.

  1. 1. Set upChoose membership, roles, and voting.
  2. 2. ContributeDefine a task, its reward, and reviewer.
  3. 3. ReviewApprove completed work and configured rewards.
  4. 4. DecideVote on supported actions and shared funds.

Source support: TaskService, MembershipAuthorityService, voting wizard routing, CreateDistributionModal, TreasuryService, tokenLabel, creation frontend, and public docs. Source inspection supports these workflows; it does not prove every live transaction succeeds today. The long-term ownership vision is broader than the software’s legal effect.

Intent boundaries prevent misleading traffic.

Legal cooperative formation requires work beyond software; the CCCD formation checklist includes bylaws, counsel, incorporation and capitalization. Election software can imply private ballots and formal assemblies, as Vocdoni’s offering illustrates. Neither is a primary target here. Broad “contribution tracking” can mean donations; broad “decentralized platform” can mean infrastructure or remote teams. Poa’s copy qualifies the organizational use.

03 / Selected plan and implementation

Improve the pages already doing useful work.

1. Explain the product plainly

Changed: homepage supporting copy now names software, its audiences, and practical actions. Task, voting, funds and joining explanations include their conditions. About copy explains the name and product. Added contextual links to ownership, voting, and treasury guides.

Preserved: emotional hero headline, established visual identity, typography, navigation, and existing product flows.

2. Match useful search intent

Changed: seven public guides and relevant navigation labels. Member voting and contribution tracking receive clearer titles and introductions. Cooperative and community examples define fit; creation and overview explain DAOs naturally. Shares replace ambiguous reward language in touched examples.

Preserved: existing URLs, detailed workflows, related links, and already useful worker-cooperative and ownership titles. No new public landing pages.

3. Remove claims broader than the product

Changed: removed universal cash-out, free-use, instant-joining, permanence, unrestricted rule-change, and no-dependence promises. Added legal and funding distinctions. Treasury figures are labeled as a historical Argus example.

Why: account type, available funding, permissions, supported actions and maintained infrastructure affect the outcome. A screenshot’s figures are not platform adoption statistics.

4. Close concrete indexing gaps

Changed: explicit noindex metadata for six legacy app redirects and error output; restored the intended noindex on the query-driven public-profile shell, where component metadata overrode the page setting; one noindex source for DocsRedirect; legacy /index.html aliases resolve to canonical docs. Expanded export checks for metadata uniqueness, internal link targets, public indexability, and indexing exclusions for application shells and aliases. Updated sitemap dates for edited guides.

Preserved: static export, prerendered public docs, canonical/sitemap architecture and structured-data approach. No rendering migration was justified.

The live legacy blog alias sample contained conflicting index/noindex meta tags. The local change uses SEOHead’s noIndex option instead of adding a second robots tag. Permanent alias handling and guard changes have regression coverage; their deployment still needs release verification.

A qualitative search sample, checked against the product.

Search results illustrate intent and language; they are not measured Google rankings or search demand. Results vary by date, engine and location.

Query sample: worker cooperative software management tools; collective decision making software members; community owned organization software; member voting software cooperative election; start worker cooperative legal formation; DAO creation governance tools Aragon Snapshot; contribution tracking rewards DAO contributors tools; decentralized organization platform governance tools; “contribution tracking” software; “participatory governance” software.

    Financial coordination and fiscal-host context. Poa does not claim fiscal sponsorship.
  8. Vocdoni — Cooperatives
    Formal assembly and private-ballot expectations.
  9. CCCD — Starting a worker cooperative
    Formation involves legal and operational work beyond creating software accounts.

Provider pages establish how providers describe their offerings. This research does not independently certify their security, legal claims, adoption or performance. No bulk search scraper was built. No paid keyword access or Search Console access was assumed; no analytics instrumentation was found during this review.

06 / After release

Measure relevant discovery and useful participation.

Discovery → first useful action

Record the release date and changed URLs. With authorized Search Console access, follow organic impressions, relevant queries, clicks, and click-through rate for each mapped page. Separate branded Poa searches from the qualified nonbranded clusters.

Observe comparable periods over several weeks and annotate seasonality or other releases. A successful build or corrected robots tag is an immediate technical result; improved search visibility requires later evidence.

No conversion instrumentation was found. Agree and implement an appropriate measurement path before claiming a funnel: public landing page → guide/create CTA → setup start → successful organization creation → first member contribution or decision. Prefer aggregate events; account addresses and task contents are unnecessary.
